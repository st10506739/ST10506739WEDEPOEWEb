Ambition is not the missing piece

Every year, thousands of young women finish school, college or university hoping to build a career or a business.

Many hit the same walls: little workplace experience, limited career guidance, and no professional network. Others have a business idea but lack the knowledge, mentorship and support to make it last.

We believe the gap is access to preparation, opportunity and support. Her Foundation exists to close it.

Read our story

Three commitments behind everything we do

We prepare

We equip young women with practical knowledge, workplace skills and entrepreneurial capabilities for long-term success.

We connect

We open access to mentors, employers, entrepreneurs, educational institutions and strategic partners.

We empower

We support young women to pursue employment, build sustainable businesses and achieve financial independence.

Two pathways to an income of her own

Employment

Workplace readiness for the jobs employers are hiring for today.

  • Career readiness and employability training
  • CV development and interview preparation
  • Workplace communication and professional etiquette
  • Digital and future-ready skills
  • Career mentorship and networking

Entrepreneurship

The mindset, skills and support to start a business that lasts.

  • Entrepreneurial mindset development
  • Business planning and enterprise development
  • Financial literacy and business management
  • Business mentorship and coaching
  • Innovation, networking and market access
