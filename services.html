Our programmes

Two pathways, one goal: a young woman earning an income of her own.

Choose a pathway

We prepare young women to enter the workforce with the practical skills, professional knowledge and workplace readiness today’s employers look for.

This pathway is for anyone preparing for a first job, returning to work or changing direction.

  • Career readiness and employability training
  • CV development and interview preparation
  • Workplace communication and professional etiquette
  • Digital and future-ready skills
  • Career mentorship and networking opportunities

We prepare aspiring entrepreneurs to build sustainable businesses that contribute to economic growth and job creation.

This pathway is for young women with a business idea, or a young business that needs structure and support.

  • Entrepreneurial mindset development
  • Business planning and enterprise development
  • Financial literacy and business management
  • Business mentorship and coaching
  • Innovation, networking and market access
