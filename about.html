About Her Foundation

A South African movement committed to preparing young women for financial independence.

Who we are

We believe financial independence is more than earning an income. It is the ability to make informed choices, build a secure future, support your family and create opportunities for others.

Across South Africa, countless young women have the ambition, talent and resilience to succeed. Many still face barriers that keep them from meaningful employment or from building a sustainable business. Her Foundation exists to bridge that gap.

Through practical skills development, mentorship, career readiness, entrepreneurship education and strategic partnerships, we prepare young women to enter the world of work with confidence or to build enterprises that create lasting economic value.

Why we exist

Financial independence remains out of reach for many young women. Some struggle to find work because of limited workplace experience, inadequate career guidance and restricted access to professional networks. Others have entrepreneurial ambition but lack the business knowledge, mentorship and support to start something that lasts.

These challenges often lead to financial insecurity, reduced economic participation and unrealised potential.

The challenge is not a lack of ambition or capability. It is limited access to preparation, opportunity and support.

Where we are headed

Our vision

A South Africa where every young woman is financially independent and empowered to thrive through employment or entrepreneurship.

Our mission

To prepare young women for financial independence with practical skills, entrepreneurial knowledge, mentorship and access to opportunities.

Our commitment

We prepare young women today so they can build stronger families, stronger businesses and a stronger South Africa tomorrow.

The future we are building

Our success is measured not by the number of programmes we deliver, but by the lives we transform. We want to see:

  • More young women securing meaningful employment
  • More women establishing sustainable businesses
  • Stronger financial resilience among young women
  • Stronger professional and entrepreneurial networks
  • Women creating opportunities for others through leadership, enterprise and mentorship

The people behind the programmes

Her Foundation runs on a small set of roles that work together.

  • Founder and director

    Sets the direction of the foundation and builds the partnerships that open doors.

  • Programme lead

    Plans and delivers the Employment and Entrepreneurship pathways.

  • Mentorship coordinator

    Matches young women with mentors and coaches and keeps those relationships on track.

Questions people ask us

Honoured, Empowered, Rooted. Each word describes how we want every young woman we work with to feel: valued for who she is, equipped to act, and grounded in her community.

Young women across South Africa who want financial independence, whether through a job or through their own business. Choose the Employment pathway, the Entrepreneurship pathway, or explore both.

You can volunteer your time, sponsor a young woman on a programme, or partner with us as a business, school, agency or community organisation. Start with the enquiry form.

We build partnerships with government, businesses, educational institutions, development agencies and community organisations that share our vision of expanding opportunities for young women.
